A 1024x1024 broadsheet front page summing up Monday 23 November 2026:
36 verified stories across 7 regions, with 13 original flat-colour drawings —
all 13 illustrated stories are genuinely Monday's news. White paper with an
ink masthead, a navy timeline band, a dotted-halftone world map on navy with
large red numbered pins at true positions, and a DATE CHECK box naming the
stories that did not happen on Monday.

## What is on the page

- Masthead: "Monday 23 November 2026", LATE EDITION, press time 16:00 UTC.
- Stat strip with the six Monday numbers 429 / −5.8% / 7 / 20+ / 10 / 239/5.
- Regions: Europe 10 · Middle East 7 · Asia 5 · Americas 8 · Africa 2 ·
  Oceania 2 · Global 2.
- The 3 Monday heroes: COP31 aftermath (Antalya), Zelenskyy — we sank the
  Merkury (Benghazi), Belgium shuts its skies (Brussels). R4 makes all 13
  illustrated stories genuinely Monday: minis 12–13 are Van Hollen's Schumer
  challenge (Washington) and Wembanyama's 50 (San Antonio).
- The 10 minis with their cities: Dymer, Gulf of Finland, Warsaw,
  Wellington, Istanbul, Paris, Herzliya, Seoul, Washington, San Antonio.
- The map: dotted-halftone continents on a navy sea, 13 red numbered pins.
- ALSO ON 14–36, DATE CHECK, and HOW WE CHECKED.
